I am an undergraduate student in Artificial Intelligence at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), graduating in 2027. My research interests lie in **large language model evaluation** and **multimodal perception**, with a focus on building evaluation systems that align with human judgment.

I am currently developing HiEval, an agent-based caption evaluation system that hierarchically explores images to assess how well machine-generated captions match human perception. I also built [IP-Bench](https://github.com/c-allergic/IP-Bench) (now IPV-Bench), the first systematic benchmark for image protection methods in image-to-video generation scenarios, as first author (arXiv:2603.26154, under review at AAAI 2027).

I spent Fall 2025 on exchange at RWTH Aachen University, and I am currently seeking research and engineering internships in AI. My CV is available [here](/cv/).
